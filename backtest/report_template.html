How this was tested

  • Strategy rules: track the highest price → when price falls a set amount below it (the dip), track the lowest price → buy when price rebounds 0.5% off that low → wide trailing stop → once price reaches the profit target, swap to a tight trailing stop → after any sale, start again with a fresh high. Long and flat only. Dollar mode (the flowchart): $60 dip, $95 wide trail, +$60 target, $10 tight trail. Percent mode: 6% dip, 9.5% wide trail, +6% target, 1% tight trail, so the distances scale with the share price.
  • Money: $10,000 starting capital, 100% of account value per trade in whole shares, 0.1% commission each side, no slippage. Buy & hold buys whole shares at the first bar's open and pays the same 0.1% commission.
  • Order fills, matching TradingView's backtester: signals are checked at each bar close, buys fill at the next bar's open, and stops fill at the stop price, or at the open if price gaps through it. Trailing stops move once per bar, never inside a bar.
  • Data: Yahoo Finance, regular trading hours only. The 4H bars are built from 1H bars on TradingView's session split (09:30–13:30, 13:30–16:00). Today's bar is unfinished: the data was fetched at .
  • Caveats: this is a Python copy of the Pine script, not TradingView's own Strategy Tester, so expect small differences. This is not investment advice.
