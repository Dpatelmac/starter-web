Top-4 unexplained losers: 3-month ledger

Each trading day from Aug 6, 2026 to Nov 3, 2026 (63 days): take the 10 biggest S&P 500 losers (down 2%+, price $20+, 1M+ shares), skip the ones with an obvious reason (earnings that day or the evening before, or an 8%+ gap down at the open), buy the 4 biggest remaining losers at the next morning's open, $1,000 each, and sell only when the trailing stop is hit. Open positions are valued at the close of Nov 4, 2026.

How it was calculated

  • Prices: Yahoo Finance daily bars, split/dividend adjusted for returns; buy and sell prices shown as traded. Earnings dates: Yahoo Finance. Missing data: NTAP.
  • Trailing stop: highest close since entry (or the entry price) × (1 − stop). If a day opens below the stop, the sale is at the open; otherwise, if the day's low touches it, at the stop price. The high-water mark updates at each close, so intraday highs don't raise the stop.
  • Costs: 0.05% per side, plus 0.10% extra slippage on stop sales. $1,000 per trade; no limit on open positions.
  • "Skip if reporting in next 10 days" uses earnings dates known before buying. "E" tags use the actual report dates during each hold.
